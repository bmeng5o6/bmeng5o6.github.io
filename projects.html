Distributed Job Scheduler ↗

A job scheduler where many worker processes pull work from a shared Postgres queue. Workers claim jobs with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, so Postgres handles mutual exclusion with no separate coordination layer. Heartbeats detect dead workers, epochs reject writes from stalled ones, and failed jobs retry with exponential backoff before being dead-lettered.

Distributed Key-Value Store ↗

A key-value store that spreads keys across nodes with a consistent hash ring and virtual nodes. Each shard has a leader that replicates to followers, a write-ahead log keeps data durable across crashes, and gossip-based failure detection routes requests to replicas when a node goes down.
